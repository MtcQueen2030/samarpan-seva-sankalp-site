01 / WHO WE ARE

Service with purpose.
Progress with people.

We believe meaningful social change starts by listening to communities and helping people access the knowledge, confidence and opportunities they need.

Samarpan Seva Sankalp (T) is building a community-oriented approach to social development. Our focus is on practical initiatives, awareness and collaboration that respect the dignity and potential of every person.
